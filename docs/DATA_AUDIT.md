## 2. Seasons / so-called "history"

- Analytical spine is **exactly 3 seasons**: 2022/23 (188124), 2023/24 (189012), 2024/25 (189974).
- 2022/23 has **no underlying raw match data** in the pipeline — standings are a fully hardcoded 20-row Wikipedia table (`fix_2022_23_standings.py`). Player stats for 22/23 do exist in the parquet.
- The 6.2 GB SQL dump contains matches for **2025/26 (191659)** and 23/24 + 24/25, but `extract_standings.py` is deliberately restricted to `{189012, 189974}`; **no script reads 191659**. A 4th (current) season is extractable from the dump.
- Valuation history is bounded **2022-07-01 → 2025-06-30** in the parquet; the supplementary TM panels (`player_valuations_full.csv`, `team_full_dossier.csv`) extend back to 2005 but are RM-scoped.
- Transfers span 2021-08 → 2026-07.

**Conclusion:** realistically usable historical seasons for training = **3** (22/23–24/25); 4th obtainable (25/26) but partially overlapping the "live" investment horizon.

## 3. Player identity audit

Two disjoint ID namespaces:
1. **Internal `player_id`** (master/features/scenarios; e.g. 4892, 675 is the Team ID of Real Madrid). Source = Transfermarkt via parquet.
2. **TM namespace** in `club_transfers.player_id`, `team_full_dossier.player_id`, `player_valuations_full.player_id`.

- Exact numeric overlap internal↔TM = **0 rows** (checked across all three supplementary files).
- Internal ids are **stable across the 3 seasons** (250 players in all 3, 258 in 2, 503 in 1; 1011 distinct players). Decline targets rely on this stability.
- **34 players appear under 2 different `team_id` in a single season** (72 rows in master, 36 duplicate `(pid, season)` key rows) — mid-season loans/moves (e.g. player 3281: Villarreal LB + Getafe LW in 24/25). These produce the master_pricing/simulation_results fan-out (§6).
- Name bridge (`_norm` = strip accents, lowercase, alnum-only): of 59 distinct `club_transfers.player_name`, only **10 resolve uniquely** to an internal `first+last`; of 52 out-transfers only 6 name-resolve. SD-DB (70 names) resolves **0**; xlsx `Cases` (137 names) resolves **0** (La Fabrica / foreign-academy pool largely absent from the 3-season LaLiga spine).
- `dossier_map` in `integrate_financials_and_la_fabrica.py` keys on `int(dossier.player_id)` (TM namespace) but looks up with the **internal** id → effectively unreachable except by accidental numeric coincidence.

**Finding:** no reliable RM↔internal identity crosswalk exists; only ~10–20% of TM-namespace entities join to the internal spine, and only via fragile name normalization.

## 4. Transfer history audit (`club_transfers.csv` — RM only)

- 105 rows: **out 52, in 53**; loans 37 (incl. 8 of 52 out); date range 2021-08-10 → 2026-07-08.
- Fee type breakdown (out 52): **34 free/undisclosed**, 18 with price > 0. price == 0: 16; price null: 56 total.
- In: 53 rows, 17 non-loan with fee.

**Usable transfer records for fee modelling:**
- `n=18` permanent out-sales with price > 0 (all 18 also have `market_value_at_transfer`). Documented comment "n=18 non-free sales" matches.
- `discount = price / mv_at_transfer` (out): median **0.884**, mean **2.83**, min **0.30** (Joselu 1.5M/5M), max **25.0** (Víctor Muñoz 25M/1M) and **6.60** (Nico Paz 66M/10M). Extreme outlier regime; the sale model's bucket-median + ridge blend + clips (0.35–1.45) exist precisely to survive it.
- Buy side: median price/mv over 17 non-loan in-transfers = **0.79**; sale pipeline's `BUY_MULTIPLIER` is hardcoded to **1.0** (i.e. pays full MV) — not derived from the observed 0.79.

**Coverage vs total transfers:** only 18/52 out deals carry a real fee; the rest are free/undisclosed/loans.

## 5. Market value audit

Parquet `player_valuations`: 8433 rows, 1132 players, 344 teams, dates 2022-07-01 → 2025-06-30 (no earlier). ~8 valuations/resident player.

Assignment semantics (`build_master_dataset.py`): for each season, keep valuations with `valuation_date` inside `[season.start_date, season.end_date]`, take the **most recent in-window** per `(player_id, team_id, season_id)`; fallback for null team_id = most recent in-window per `(player, season)`.

**Coverage in master by season:**
| Season | rows | MV non-null |
|---|---|---|
| 188124 | 596 | 441 (74.0%) |
| 189012 | 608 | 466 (76.6%) |
| 189974 | 601 | 489 (81.4%) |
| **total** | 1805 | **1396 (77.3%)** |

Missing MV is **concentrated in relegated/promoted clubs** (Espanyol 77, Elche 34, Almería 25, Las Palmas 25, Cádiz 23, Atlético 20, Valladolid 19, Rayo 18 …). `market_value_m` coverage 77.3% corroborated in `features_with_financials`; `market_value_at_checkpoint` = 0% (never populated).

**Semantic caution:** the in-window latest = a "season snapshot" at the last valuation date inside the season (usually near season end), NOT a canonical start/end-of-season value; and `market_value_at_transfer` (TM) is a transfer-day price-adjacent MV sometimes quoted after the deal. Small positive look-ahead risk in MV-dependent features is present but bounded.

## 6. Row-coherence divergence: 1805 vs 1877 (IMPORTANT)

All files were regenerated together (timestamps 21:13:33–21:16:43), so the two 1877-row outputs are **not stale**; they are a structural fan-out:

- `master_dataset` = 1805 (36 dup `(pid, season)` keys = 34 two-club-in-a-season players).
- `interim/master_pricing.csv` = **1877** (72 dup `(pid, team, season)`, unique `(pid, team, season)` = 1805 = master). The financials merge (`extend_master_pricing.py`) expands the 72 two-team rows.
- `processed/simulation_results.csv` = **1877**, unique `(pid, season_name)` < 1877 — simulated per player-season then inflated by the same dual-membership rows.

Practical impact: RM 24/25 (30 rows, no dual memberships here) is unaffected, but any cross-club aggregation will over-count the 34 dual-membership players; dashboard/next-gen code should dedupe to `(player_id, team_id, season_id)` keys consistently.

## 7. Contract / wage / financial coverage

- `master_pricing.csv` (`extend_master_pricing.py`, applied to **all 1877 rows**, all clubs):
  - `annual_wage_eur` = `MV × 0.045` × position factor × age factor, clipped [150k, 25M] → **100% non-null, entirely heuristic**.
  - `contract_years_remaining` = **constant 3.0** (single distinct value) → 100% non-null, entirely heuristic.
  - `predicted_sale_fee` = bucket-median × ridge × age/pos heuristics, clip [0.35,1.45]×MV.
  - `sale_discount` null for 23% (MV missing).
- Real fee data coverage in `features_with_financials`: `purchase_fee_m` **0.8%** (14/1805), `last_sale_fee_m` **0.9%** (16/1805), `market_value_m` 77.3%. RM 24/25 (30 rows): purchase 16.7%, sale 3.3%, MV 76.7%.
- `club_financial_metrics.csv`: 6 rows, **Real Madrid only**, annual checkpoints; 110 cols of portfolio-style ratios (CAGR, Sharpe, Sortino, VaR, CVaR, Calmar …) — no wage bills, no fee-flow data, no other club.
- xG/xA: `stat_total_xg_shot`, `stat_total_xg_assist`, `stat_total_xg_save` (and per90/average variants) **are present in master but used NOWHERE** in `src/` (grep = 0 hits).

**Conclusion:** financial layer is ~99% synthetic. Real economics = only 34 out of 1805 rows have any observed fee.

## 8. Sporting layer consumption (what the models actually use)

- `sporting_risk_model`: target `decline` = next-season GC drop; features = all `stat_total_*` + percentages + derived (GC p90, shot conversion, minutes utilization, age/position risks…). Train 22/23→23/24, validate 23/24→24/25. 24/25 rows have **no target** (fitted but unusable as labels).
- `club_sporting_model`: attack = `[total_goal_contributions, total_shots, total_assists]` → GF; defence = `[total_market_value, total_interceptions, count_DF, squad_minutes_utilization, count_GK, avg_age]` → GA (note: **MV feeds the defence model**). xP via independent Poisson vs league-average opponent, then attack-only delta. Reference season hardcoded 24/25.
- Scenario engine: uniform `market_value` scaling 0.95 for raise; depth floors GK≥2/DF≥6/MF≥6/FW≥4; caps 3000 scenarios / 9000 signings.
- Simulation: pure age/decline grow+gaussian noise on MV; `performance_delta` hardcoded 0 → MV sim is **not** performance-linked.

## 9. Temporal leakage scan

- Decline target uses `shift(-1)` — label from the *following* season; train/val split is chronological (no leak).
- MV selection is latest *within* the season window — acceptable.
- `market_value_at_transfer` is intrinsically deal-day MV (used only by the sale model at inference on the same semantics as training, so consistent, though TM quoting may post-date the tweet-level announcement).
- **No season 191659 anywhere in src**; no unsupported look-ahead was found. Residual risk: RM-only fee model freely applied to all-club pricing rows (§10).

## 10. Data-quality risks (ranked)

1. **Identity gap** — 2 id namespaces, no crosswalk, ≤20% joinable (name-only).
2. **Fee scarcity** — 18 RM observations; *zero* fee reality for other 23 clubs; wage/contract 100% heuristic (3y, 4.5% MV).
3. **Fan-out duplication** — 72-row inflation in master_pricing/simulation_results from dual-club player-seasons.
4. **Scope mismatch** — RM-trained sale-discount model / RM-only `club_financial_metrics` vs priced rows for all clubs; `BUY_MULTIPLIER=1.0` vs observed 0.79; `dossier_map` keyed in the wrong id namespace.
5. **MV bias** — ~23% missing, concentrated where players are cheapest (relegated/promoted clubs); 22/23 standings synthetic.
6. **Unused asset** — xG/xA present but ignored by the sporting models.
7. **Season depth** — only 3 seasons; 25/26 extractable but overlaps the live horizon.

## 12. Top-5 data gaps (drives DATA_SCHEMA_PLAN.md)

1. **Observed transfer economics** for all 24 LaLiga clubs (fee, fee-type, loan terms, deal date) — not just RM.
2. **Identity registry** bridging TM id ↔ internal id ↔ normalized name (with club+season context).
3. **Contract/wage truth** (wage, contract-length) — currently 100% heuristic.
4. **Season & valuation history depth** (pre-2022, and out-of-league players sold abroad so sales can be priced).
5. **MV completeness** for relegated/promoted and mid-season dual-club players (plus canonical start/end-of-season snapshots).
